# Content and copy

- **Language.** Interface copy is Ukrainian. Write it plainly and functionally: a service is named by what it is — «Штрафи», «Петиції», «ЖКП», «Паркування», «Укриття».
- **Voice.** Neutral and civic, informative rather than chatty. Greetings are short and warm («Доброго дня!»), everything else is instruction or status. No exclamation marks outside the greeting, no marketing language, no emoji.
- **Casing.** Sentence case for titles, buttons and labels («Оплатити штраф», not «Оплатити Штраф»). Month names are lowercase in Ukrainian: «23 жовтня».
- **Numbers and data.** Amounts as `1 250,00 ₴` (space for thousands, comma for decimals, ₴ after a space). Phone numbers as `+380 44 123 45 67`. Licence plates and card numbers are shown in full width with their own spacing (`АА 1234 ВВ`, `1234 5678 9012`). Time in 24 hours: `14:20`.
- **Actions.** Buttons say what happens, verb first: «Оплатити», «Підписати петицію», «Додати авто». Avoid «ОК» and «Так» when a verb fits.
- **Errors.** Say what went wrong and what to do, in one line under the field: «Невірний номер. Перевірте формат: АА 1234 ВВ».

These are the short rules for screens. The full copywriting and tone-of-voice rules are the section «Копірайтинг і тон голосу» (`copywriting.md`): brand voice and tone by situation, address (lowercase «ви»), the mandatory glossary (застосунок, not «додаток»; транспортна карта vs банківська картка; QR-квиток; паркінг), text structure, UI patterns, errors, accessibility labels, push notifications and localization. Read it before writing any interface copy; where the two differ, that section wins.
