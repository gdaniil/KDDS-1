The short-lived status message on Liquid Glass. Fills its container; text wraps.

**You provide** `type` (`success` with a green check, `error` with a red cross) and `label`.

- One short sentence: «Штраф сплачено», «Не вдалося оплатити. Спробуйте ще раз».
- **Always at the top** of the screen: right under the status bar (over the navbar), `space-4` from the side edges, over the content. Never at the bottom, where it collides with the bottom search, pinned actions, the keyboard and the home indicator. Dismiss it after a few seconds in app code.
- The icon carries the status with the words; never rely on colour alone.
